# POLYBOT V2 Neural Mesh Part 2A Brain Output Contract

## 1. Purpose

V2 Neural Mesh Part 2A creates the canonical Brain Output language for POLYBOT.

Signals are neutral facts from neurons. Brain Outputs are structured interpretations over Signals or other evidence. They are advisory only and do not execute trades.

## 2. Why Brain Outputs Need A Shared Language

POLYBOT has multiple brain surfaces: context, opportunity, risk, capital, exit, no-trade, AI, strategy, memory, and execution advisory. Without one shared output contract, later coordination cannot see dependencies, conflicts, stale interpretations, or missing evidence consistently.

This phase stores those interpretations in one persistence-backed format.

## 3. Signals vs Brain Outputs vs Future Coordinator Decisions

Signal:

Neutral structured information produced by a neuron.

Brain Output:

An interpretation, warning, hint, note, or advisory generated by a brain.

Future Coordinator Decision:

A later phase that may combine Brain Outputs. It is not implemented here.

## 6. Dependency Model

Dependencies are stored in `brain_output_dependencies`.

Dependency fields:

- `brain_output_id`
- `dependency_type`
- `dependency_id`
- `dependency_role`
- `confidence`
- `created_at`

Supported dependency types:

- `signal`
- `brain_output`
- `event`
- `source`

Conflict fields:

- `brain_output_id`

Conflict severity is numeric `0..1` when provided.

## 12. Safety Rules

- Brain Outputs are advisory/interpretive only.
- Brain Outputs do not place orders.
- Brain Outputs do not cancel orders.
- Brain Outputs do not sign requests.
- Brain Outputs do not bypass State Governor or Risk Gate.
- Brain Outputs do not imply approval for trade.
- Executable recommendation values like `BUY`, `SELL`, `ENTER_TRADE`, and `PLACE_ORDER` are rejected.
- Executable/order metadata keys are rejected.
- No secrets are stored or exposed.
- Empty output store is valid and reported truthfully.
